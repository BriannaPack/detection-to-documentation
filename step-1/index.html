Install the extension

Add the free ‘Revision History’ extension to Chrome on your laptop, pin it, and sign in, so you can follow along in your own Google Doc.

Now try it

On your laptop, type revisionhistory.com into Chrome and click Add to Chrome. Pin it to your toolbar, open any Google Doc, and click Sign in with Google in the bar at the top.
