Using a ‘Revision History’ extension to support authentic student writing in the age of AI

Make your students SHOW their work

Detection guesses from the product. Documentation shows the process.

AI detectors can’t show how the work was done, and they flag some students far more than others. The free ‘Revision History’ extension for Chrome replays how a Google Doc was written, so you can design assignments where the writing process is the evidence.

SHOW in four parts

Structure the assignment

Set up milestones in one shared Doc, so you can see the writing process.

Hold students accountable

Enforce the requirements, like no pasting, so the process stays visible.

Observe and respond

Give feedback on the process at each milestone, so students improve.

Weigh the whole picture

Grade the whole process, not just the final product.

Steps 1 to 3 show you the tool on your own Google Doc. Step 4 puts SHOW into practice.
